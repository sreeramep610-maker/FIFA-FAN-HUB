France Eyes Another World Cup

Depth, quality and attacking power made France one of the biggest stories of the 2026 FIFA World Cup — from strong group-stage performances to a fourth-place finish.

01 — The Legacy

A Nation Built for the Biggest Stage

France arrived at the 2026 World Cup with one of the strongest recent records in international football.

From 1998 to 2026

France won the World Cup in 1998 and again in 2018. They reached the final in 2022, then returned to the final four in 2026.

The 2026 campaign ended in fourth place after a semi-final defeat to Spain and a bronze-final defeat to England.

France entered the tournament with the expectation of competing for the biggest prize.

03 — The Attack

Mbappé, Dembélé and Olise Lead the Revolution

France's attacking approach became one of the defining features of their 2026 campaign.

Three Group-Stage Wins

France defeated Senegal 3–1, Iraq 3–0 and Norway 4–1, scoring ten goals in the group stage. Mbappé and Dembélé were major attacking contributors, while Olise provided creativity.

3 Group wins

The New Attacking Identity

Rather than relying only on compact defending and fast transitions, France showed a willingness to put more attacking players on the pitch and create pressure higher up the field.

More creativity. More movement. More attacking options.

05 — The Coach

Didier Deschamps: The End of an Era

The 2026 tournament marked the end of Didier Deschamps' long spell in charge of Les Bleus.

A Remarkable International Record

Deschamps guided France to the 2018 World Cup title, the 2022 final and the 2021 UEFA Nations League title. In 2026 he attempted to maximise France's extraordinary attacking talent.

His final tournament ended after the semi-final defeat to Spain.

06 — The Semi-Final

France vs Spain

The dream of another World Cup final ended in Dallas when Spain defeated France 2–0.

Spain
2 — 0
SEMI-FINAL • DALLAS
France

Spain Take Control

Mikel Oyarzabal opened the scoring from the penalty spot before Pedro Porro doubled Spain's advantage. France were unable to find the attacking rhythm that had carried them through the earlier rounds.

A Final Farewell

Didier Deschamps applauded the French supporters after the final whistle, bringing his long international coaching era to a close.

07 — Bronze Final

France vs England: A Ten-Goal Thriller

France's final match became one of the most dramatic games of the competition.

England
6 — 4
BRONZE FINAL • MIAMI
France

France Fight Back

England built a huge lead, but France launched a remarkable comeback. Kylian Mbappé scored twice, Bradley Barcola added another and Ousmane Dembélé scored deep into stoppage time. England held on for a 6–4 win and third place.

11 — Legacy

The Future of Les Bleus

The trophy was out of reach, but the future remains bright.

France leave the 2026 World Cup without a medal, but their semi-final appearance confirms the strength of their footballing system. The squad contains an extraordinary mixture of established stars and young players ready to take the next step.

Mbappé remains the central figure. Dembélé offers experience and creativity, while Olise, Barcola, Doué and Cherki represent the future. With the Deschamps era over, a new chapter begins.
