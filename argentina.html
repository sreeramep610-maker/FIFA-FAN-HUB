The Scaloni Era

Lionel Scaloni became one of the defining figures of Argentina's modern football era, combining tactical discipline with a strong team identity.

Argentina's approach mixed possession, pressing, defensive organisation and quick transitions. The team could slow a match down or suddenly increase the tempo.
